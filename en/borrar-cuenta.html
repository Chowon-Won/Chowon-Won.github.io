Delete your VIXEN account

You can delete your account and all its content whenever you want.

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open VIXEN and go to Profile.
  2. Tap the Settings gear.
  3. Tap Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app

Write to virav.soporte@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Delete my VIXEN account” and your username. We will delete it within 30 days at most and confirm it to you.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email and password.
  • Your profile: name, bio and photo.
  • Your posts, comments, likes, followers and following.
  • Your Store resources.
  • Your private drawing cloud, your reports, blocks and favorites.

Drawings saved on your phone are not touched: they are yours and stay on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app. The server's technical logs disappear on their own within a few days.
