Terms of use

Last updated: October 21, 2026

By using VIXEN (by ViRav Studio) you accept these terms. If you don't agree, don't use the app.

1. The app

VIXEN is a drawing app. The editor works without an account. The community, the resource store and the cloud require a free account.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old.
  • You are responsible for what is done with your account. Keep your password safe.
  • You can delete your account whenever you want (Profile → Settings → Delete my account).

3. Your content is yours

Your drawings and resources remain yours. By publishing them on VIXEN you give us a free, non-exclusive permission to store, show and distribute them within the app while they remain published, only so the service can work. You can withdraw them at any time by deleting them.

Store resources are shared under the license chosen by their creator (public domain, credit the author, personal use or commercial use). Anyone who downloads a resource must respect that license.

4. Community guidelines

  • Only publish what you created yourself or have the right to share. No tracing or stolen art.
  • Respect others: no insults, harassment, threats or hate speech.
  • No explicit sexual content, graphic violence or anything that puts minors at risk.
  • No spam, scams, impersonation or misleading advertising.
  • Don't try to get around the service's security or use other people's accounts.

You can report and block from the ⋯ menu of each post, comment, resource or profile. Content with several reports is hidden until it is reviewed. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these guidelines.

5. Ads and payments

The app shows Google AdMob ads outside the editor and offers the optional VIXEN Pro subscription (no ads). All Store resources are free: no resources are sold in VIXEN.

Portfolio and commissions. Every artist can show their work and their social accounts (only X, Instagram and TikTok). Verified artists can show guide prices for their commissions. VIXEN does not sell, take a commission or take part in those agreements: the work, payment and delivery are agreed directly between the artist and the client, outside the app, and are the responsibility of both. If you suspect a scam, report the portfolio.

VIXEN is provided “as is”. We do our best to make it work well and keep you from losing your work, but we can't guarantee there will be no failures or interruptions. We recommend exporting copies of your important drawings.
