Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the VIXEN app (by ViRav Studio, Dominican Republic) processes, what for, who it is shared with and how you can delete it. Contact: virav.soporte@gmail.com.

In short
  • Your drawings are saved only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you ask.
  • If you create an account, we store your email and profile details so the community can work.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads, which may use your device's advertising ID.
  • We don't sell your data. You can delete your account and all its content from the app.

1. Data we process

Without an account: you can draw without signing up. Your drawings, brushes and settings are saved in your phone's storage and are not sent to any server.

With an account (only if you choose to create one):

  • Account data: email address and password (stored encrypted; we never see it), username, display name, bio and profile photo.
  • What you publish: images of your drawings, titles, descriptions, tags, comments, likes, who you follow, resources you share in the Store and their previews. All of this is public within VIXEN.
  • Your private cloud: the drawings you choose to save to the cloud (up to 50 MB). Only you can see them.
  • Safety activity: reports you make, users you block and favorite articles. These are private.
  • Technical data: for a limited time the server logs data such as your IP address, the date and the type of request, for security and to fix errors.

2. What we use it for

  • Creating and maintaining your account, and sending you the confirmation or password recovery code.
  • Showing your profile, posts and resources to other users, and keeping your private cloud.
  • Moderating the community (reports, blocks) and preventing abuse and spam.
  • Showing the ads that fund the app.

The legal basis is your consent when you create the account and use its features, and our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

VIXEN shows a Google AdMob banner on the home screen (never inside the editor). Google may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address and technical device data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, and in some cases to personalize them. In countries where the law requires it, the app shows you a Google consent form.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or ask for non-personalized ads, in your phone's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

4. Who we share it with

We use these providers, which process the data on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: server, database, file storage and accounts. The servers may be located outside the Dominican Republic (for example, in the United States).
  • Brevo: sending the emails with codes.
  • Google AdMob: ads (see section 3).

We don't sell or rent your data. We would only hand it over to an authority if the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as you have the account. If you delete a post, a comment, a resource or a drawing from the cloud, it is removed from the service immediately. If you delete your account, your profile and all your content are deleted. Technical logs are kept for only a few days.

6. Your rights

  • See and correct your data: from Profile → Edit profile.
  • Delete your account and all its content: from the app (Profile → Settings → Delete my account) or by asking by email. See Delete my account.
  • Request a copy of your data or object to any use: write to us at virav.soporte@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days at most.

These rights are offered in accordance with Law 172-13 on the protection of personal data of the Dominican Republic and, where applicable, other laws that protect you.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). On every operation the server checks that you can only see and change what belongs to you: for example, nobody else can see your private cloud or edit your profile. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your data.

8. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. VIXEN is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is important, we will let you know in the app.
